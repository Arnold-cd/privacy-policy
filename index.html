Privacy Policy for Matam

Effective Date: August 13, 2026 | Last Updated: August 13, 2026

Welcome to Matam ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application Matam.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use the app:

  • Account & Authentication Information: If you sign in via Google Sign-In, we collect your name, email address, and profile picture URL provided by Google to manage your account session.
  • User-Generated Content: Information you enter locally or synchronize, such as family tree nodes (Sagei Family Tree) and custom task checklists.
  • Device & Usage Data: Device hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and anonymous performance logs.

2. Third-Party Services

The app integrates third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services & Google Sign-In: Authentication and user identity management.
  • Google AdMob: AdMob uses Advertising IDs (AAID/IDFA) to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. Pro subscribers enjoy an entirely ad-free experience with AdMob disabled.
  • Supabase Cloud: Secure database hosting for authentication and user subscriptions.
  • Google Play Store Billing: Handles in-app purchases and subscriptions securely without storing financial data on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain application features (Clan Classifier, Match-Maker, Family Tree).
  • To manage user authentication and account status (Pro vs. Free Tier).
  • To display relevant advertising via Google AdMob (for non-Pro users).
  • To respond to user inquiries and technical support requests.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Your local data remains on your device. Account profile data stored in Supabase is retained only as long as necessary to provide service. You have the right to request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child under 13, we will delete that information immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
